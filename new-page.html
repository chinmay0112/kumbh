THE SACRED JOURNEY

A Sacred CycleTwelve Yearsin the Making

Every twelve years, Nashik and Trimbakeshwar become the meeting place of millions of pilgrims, saints, seekers and visitors. Guided by an ancient tradition and centred around the sacred Godavari, the Kumbh is a celebration of faith, heritage and the timeless spirit of India.

STORIES OF FAITH

The Sacred Cycle Returns

After twelve years, the Godavari awaits once more. What draws millions back to its shores?

READ STORY→

For a pilgrim, returning to the river can be a return to family memories, shared prayers and a sense of belonging. The journey begins long before the first step onto the ghats.

At the water's edge, individual journeys become a shared moment of faith. The river connects those arriving for the first time with those carrying a lifetime of memories.

STORIES OF SAINTS

Akhara Processions Through the Ages

How the ancient spiritual orders have carried tradition through centuries of Kumbh.

READ STORY→

The sight of saffron robes, ceremonial banners and gathered ascetics is among the enduring images of the Kumbh. These processions bring spiritual communities into the shared life of the gathering.

Beyond the spectacle lies a quieter story of teachers and disciples, daily practice and traditions passed from one generation to the next.

STORIES OF NASHIK

The City That Lives for the Kumbh

Nashik transforms itself every twelve years - and the transformation begins long before the first pilgrim arrives.

READ STORY→

A pilgrimage is also a meeting with a city: its riverside lanes, temple courtyards, shopkeepers and residents. In Nashik, these everyday places become part of each visitor's experience of the Kumbh.

Behind the great gathering are countless small acts of welcome. Directions offered, food shared and space made for a traveller become stories carried home long after the journey ends.
